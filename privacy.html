Overview

EnduroPace is designed as a privacy-first endurance planning and performance app. No account or login is required to use the app.

Data Collection

EnduroPace does not transmit your calculator data, saved plans, Race Day Plans, Performance Lab measurements, or app settings to the developer.

EnduroPace does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or third-party data brokers.

Local Storage

EnduroPace calculations are performed locally on your device.

Information that may be stored locally on your device includes:

  • Run, Bike, and Swim calculator state.
  • Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon calculator state.
  • Saved calculator plans.
  • Race Day Planner plans, including race details and pacing strategy.
  • Run threshold pace measurements.
  • Bike FTP measurements.
  • Swim CSS measurements.
  • Performance Lab measurement history.
  • App settings and unit preferences.

This locally stored information is used to support app functionality and is not transmitted to an EnduroPace server.

Deleting Local Data

Saved calculator plans can be cleared from Settings within EnduroPace. Race Day Plans can be deleted from Race Day Planner.

Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored EnduroPace data, subject to normal iOS device and backup behavior.

Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

EnduroPace does not use third-party advertising services or third-party analytics services within the app.
