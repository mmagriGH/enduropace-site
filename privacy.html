Effective date: August 2026

Overview

EnduroPace is designed as a privacy-first endurance pace planning app. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal data.

Data Collection

EnduroPace does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Local Storage

Calculations happen locally on your device. Saved run and bike plans are stored locally on your device using device storage. This information is not sent to a server.

Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

EnduroPace does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.
