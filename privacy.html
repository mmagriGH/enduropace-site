Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Private by design.

EnduroPace is designed around a local-first planning and performance experience. No account or login is required to use the app.

Local EnduroPace Data

App settings, calculator state, saved plans, Race Day Plans, Performance Lab measurements, Sweat Tests, race checklists, nutrition strategies, race-plan history, recommendation state, and related EnduroPace-owned data are stored in the app's local persistence stores on your device.

EnduroPace is designed so that its deterministic calculators and core locally saved planning data do not require continuous internet access.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and read-only. EnduroPace reads only the Health data types you choose to make available to the app. It does not write workouts or measurements back to Apple Health.

EnduroPace does not duplicate raw workout telemetry, heart-rate streams, or GPS tracks into its own durable stores. Apple Health observations remain read-only unless you explicitly choose to adopt a supported observation as an EnduroPace performance measurement.

When you adopt a supported measurement, EnduroPace preserves source, measurement date, import date, and related provenance so the value can be distinguished from manually entered or EnduroPace-measured data.

Apple Weather and Saved Race Locations

When you request race weather, EnduroPace uses the verified race location and race date saved with that event to request forecast or historical temperature information from Apple Weather.

Weather-derived suggestions are presented for review. EnduroPace does not silently replace athlete-entered race conditions or race-plan decisions.

Apple Maps Race Imagery

Automatic race backgrounds can use the verified race location saved with an event to request scenic imagery through Apple Maps services. This feature uses the location associated with the saved race rather than your device's current location.

EnduroPace may cache generated race imagery locally to improve performance.

Custom Race Photos

If you choose a custom race background, EnduroPace receives access only to the image you select through Apple's photo picker and saves an app-local copy for that race.

EnduroPace does not upload your selected custom race photo.

Apple Intelligence Coach

On supported devices, EnduroPace Pro can use Apple's on-device intelligence to generate natural-language explanations from deterministic EnduroPace coaching facts and recommendation context.

EnduroPace calculations and deterministic engines remain the source of truth. Generated wording does not silently change performance measurements, race plans, fueling, hydration, sodium, or other athlete decisions.

Subscriptions and Purchases

EnduroPace Pro subscriptions, introductory-offer eligibility, purchases, restores, and subscription management are handled through Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.

EnduroPace receives the product and entitlement information required to determine Pro access. EnduroPace does not receive your payment-card details.

Optional Anonymous Usage Analytics

Anonymous product-usage analytics are off by default. You can enable or disable them at any time in EnduroPace Settings.

When enabled, EnduroPace sends product-usage events through TelemetryDeck to help improve the app. EnduroPace filters custom event properties through a strict allowlist before they are sent.

Custom event properties do not include race names, saved race locations, race dates and times, body data, performance values, Apple Health values, fuel product names, notes, contact information, or user identifiers.

Data Control and Deletion

You can manage or delete saved plans, Race Day Plans, Performance Lab measurements, Sweat Tests, strategies, and other supported local data from their corresponding areas of the app.

Removing EnduroPace from your device may also remove locally stored app data, subject to normal iOS device and backup behavior.

Internet Access

Network access is used for features such as Apple Weather, Apple Maps race imagery, App Store subscription transactions and restores, EnduroPace web and support resources, and optional analytics when enabled.

Advertising, Tracking, and Data Sales

EnduroPace does not include advertising networks, does not use app data for cross-app advertising tracking, and does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

EnduroPace does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as EnduroPace changes. Material privacy changes will be reflected in the published policy and, when appropriate, in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@gamacoraggio.com.

EnduroPace provides planning and performance tools for informational purposes and is not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, or coaching advice.

EnduroPace is developed and published by GamaCoraggio LLC.

EnduroPace technology is patent pending in the United States.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Private by design.

EnduroPace is designed around a local-first planning and performance experience. No account or login is required to use the current app experience.

Local Data

Core calculator state, app settings, saved calculator plans, Race Day Plans, and Performance Lab measurements are designed to use the app’s local persistence stores on your device.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Run, Bike, Swim, Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon calculator state.
  • Saved calculator plans.
  • Race Day Plans, including pacing, transitions, conditions, fueling, hydration, sodium, and timeline planning.
  • Run Threshold Pace, Bike FTP, Swim CSS, and Sweat Test measurements.
  • Performance Lab measurement history.
  • App settings, Default Activity, and unit preferences.
Core planning and performance values are not included in EnduroPace’s optional anonymous usage analytics events.

Data Control and Deletion

You control the data saved by the app. Saved calculator plans can be cleared from Settings, Race Day Plans can be deleted from Race Day Planner, and Performance Lab measurements and saved Sweat Tests can be managed from their corresponding Performance Lab areas.

Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored EnduroPace data, subject to normal iOS device and backup behavior.

Optional Anonymous Usage Analytics

Anonymous product-usage analytics are off by default. You can enable or disable them at any time in EnduroPace Settings.

When enabled, EnduroPace sends product-usage events through TelemetryDeck to help improve the app. EnduroPace filters custom event properties through a strict allowlist before they are sent.

Custom event properties do not include race names, locations, dates and times, body data, performance values, fuel product names, plan notes, contact information, or user identifiers.

Internet Access

Core calculations and locally saved planning data do not require continuous internet access.

Internet access is used when you open EnduroPace web resources, support or privacy pages, App Store links, and when optional analytics are enabled.

Advertising and Data Sales

EnduroPace does not include advertising networks in the current app and does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Children’s Privacy

The current EnduroPace app does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as EnduroPace changes. Material privacy changes will be reflected in the published policy and, when appropriate, in the app.
