Race Day Planner

Race Day Planner brings race details, pacing, conditions, transitions, fueling, hydration, sodium, and the execution timeline together in one connected plan.

If something appears incomplete, review the race details and Performance Lab inputs connected to the plan. The Home screen’s readiness and Next Best Action can also point you toward missing planning steps.

Settings & Saved Plans

Open More → Settings to manage units, Default Activity, anonymous usage analytics, and calculator reset.

Saved calculator plans can be managed from Plans. Race Day Plans can be managed from Race Day Planner.

Quick Troubleshooting

  1. Confirm your selected activity, distance, units, and entered values.
  2. Check whether a required Performance Lab measurement or Sweat Test is missing.
  3. If a calculator appears stuck, use More → Settings → Reset All Calculators.
  4. Close and reopen the app if the interface does not refresh as expected.
  5. If the problem persists, contact support with the details listed above.

Privacy

No account is required for the current app experience. Core planning data is designed to remain local on your device. Optional anonymous usage analytics are off by default and can be controlled from Settings.

Run, Bike & Swim

EnduroPace provides dedicated calculators for Run, Bike, and Swim. Depending on the sport, you can calculate combinations of distance, time, pace, or speed, create splits, review estimates, and save plans.

Brick, Duathlon & Triathlon

Multisport calculators let you plan each discipline independently, include transitions, use common race-distance presets or custom distances, and calculate your complete target finish time.

Performance Lab

Performance Lab lets you record and review key endurance performance anchors: Run threshold pace, Bike FTP, and Swim CSS.

EnduroPace uses these measurements to display sport-specific training zones and measurement history.

Race Day Planner

Race Day Planner combines your race details and multisport pacing strategy. You can save race name, location, race date, race type, discipline targets, transition times, and target finish time.

Saved Plans & Settings

Saved calculator plans can be revisited from EnduroPace and cleared from Settings. Race Day Plans can be managed from Race Day Planner.

Unit preferences for Run, Bike, and Swim can also be changed from Settings.

Privacy

No account is required. Calculations happen locally on your device, and EnduroPace is designed to store app settings, saved plans, Race Day Plans, and Performance Lab measurements locally.

Important Note

EnduroPace is a planning tool. Actual results may vary based on fitness, training, terrain, weather, elevation, equipment, fueling, hydration, fatigue, and race-day conditions.
