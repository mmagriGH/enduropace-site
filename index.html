Version 1.5

Race smarter with connected endurance intelligence.

EnduroPace brings pacing, Performance Lab measurements, race conditions, hydration, sodium, fueling, race execution, and connected athlete data into one local-first planning experience.

Version 1.5 introduces EnduroPace Pro with deeper performance history, Apple Health integration, race weather, advanced fueling and hydration intelligence, reusable nutrition strategies, Race Plan History, and the Apple Intelligence Coach on supported devices.

What’s new in 1.5

  • EnduroPace Pro with annual and monthly subscriptions and a 3-day introductory trial when eligible.
  • Apple Intelligence Coach that explains deterministic EnduroPace insights in natural language on supported devices.
  • Apple Health read-only integration with explicit adoption, source provenance, freshness, and confidence.
  • Race Weather using a verified saved race location and Apple Weather, with suggestions presented for review rather than silently applied.
  • Advanced Performance, Hydration, and Fueling Intelligence, including history, trend context, best-match guidance, and reusable race nutrition strategies.
  • Race Plan History and plan-impact review so important upstream changes can be reviewed before they affect race execution.
  • Race Checklist, location-aware race imagery, sport artwork, and optional custom race photos.

Private by design

EnduroPace does not require an account. EnduroPace-owned planning, performance, strategy, history, and custom-photo data are designed to remain local to the app unless a feature explicitly uses an Apple service.

Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Anonymous product analytics are off by default. EnduroPace does not include advertising networks and does not use app data for cross-app advertising tracking.

About EnduroPace

EnduroPace is an endurance race-planning and performance app for running, cycling, swimming, triathlon, duathlon, and brick workouts.

The core experience remains useful without Pro. EnduroPace Pro adds deeper history, personalization, connected data, advanced intelligence, reusable strategies, and richer race-planning workflows.
