Plan. Perform. Progress.

EnduroPace helps runners, cyclists, swimmers, and multisport athletes turn distance, time, pace, speed, and performance targets into a clear plan.

Calculate individual sports, build Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon strategies, track key performance anchors, and organize your next race with Race Day Planner.

Run

Calculate pace, finish time, and distance. Build splits, review pace zones and race estimates, and save run plans.

Bike

Calculate speed, ride time, and distance. Build bike splits, compare ride estimates, and save bike plans.

Swim

Calculate swim pace, time, and distance in meters or yards. Build standard or custom splits and save swim plans.

Multisport

Plan Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon events with independent discipline targets, transitions, race presets, custom distances, and complete target finish times.

Performance Lab

Record Run threshold pace, Bike FTP, and Swim CSS. Review sport-specific training zones and measurement history.

Race Day Planner

Combine your race details, multisport pacing strategy, transitions, and target finish time into one saved race-day plan.

Privacy First

EnduroPace does not require an account. Calculations happen locally on your device, and app settings, saved plans, Race Day Plans, and Performance Lab measurements are designed to remain stored locally on your device.

EnduroPace is a planning tool. Actual results may vary based on fitness, training, terrain, weather, elevation, equipment, fueling, hydration, fatigue, and race-day conditions.

About EnduroPace

EnduroPace helps endurance athletes plan running and cycling efforts with pace, speed, finish time, estimated distance, splits, training zones, race estimates, and saved plans.

Run Planning

Calculate pace, finish time, estimated distance, splits, pace zones, race estimates, and same-pace finish times.

Bike Planning

Calculate average speed, ride time, estimated distance, bike splits, and same-speed ride estimates.

Privacy First

EnduroPace does not require an account. Calculations happen locally on your device. Saved plans are stored locally on your device.

EnduroPace is a planning tool. Actual results may vary based on training, terrain, weather, elevation, equipment, fueling, fatigue, and race-day conditions.
