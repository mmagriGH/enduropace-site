About EnduroPace

EnduroPace is an endurance race-planning and performance app for running, cycling, swimming, triathlon, duathlon, and brick workouts. It combines pacing, Performance Lab measurements, race conditions, hydration, sodium, fueling, and race-day timeline planning.

Support

For support questions, feedback, or issues with EnduroPace, contact mmagri@gmail.com.

When contacting support, include a brief description of the issue and the device/iOS version you are using. Please do not send sensitive personal or health information unless it is necessary for your support request.

Privacy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

EnduroPace is designed around a local-first experience. App settings, saved calculator plans, Race Day Plans, and Performance Lab measurements are stored using the app's local persistence stores on your device.

EnduroPace does not require an account to use the current app experience. You control saved plans and can delete them from the app.

Optional Anonymous Usage Analytics

Anonymous product-usage analytics are off by default. You can enable or disable them at any time in EnduroPace settings.

When enabled, EnduroPace sends product-usage events through TelemetryDeck to help improve the app. EnduroPace filters custom event properties through a strict allowlist before they are sent. Custom event properties do not include race names, locations, dates and times, body data, performance values, fuel product names, notes, contact information, or user identifiers.

Internet Access

Core calculations and locally saved planning data do not require continuous internet access. Internet access is required when you open EnduroPace web resources, support/privacy links, App Store links, and when optional analytics are enabled.

Advertising

EnduroPace does not include advertising networks in the current app.

Children's Privacy

The current EnduroPace app does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This page may be updated as EnduroPace changes. Material privacy changes will be reflected in the published policy and, when appropriate, in the app.

EnduroPace provides planning and performance tools for informational purposes and is not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, or coaching advice.
