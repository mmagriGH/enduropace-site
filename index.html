Plan. Perform. Progress.

EnduroPace helps runners, cyclists, swimmers, and multisport athletes turn distance, time, pace, speed, and performance targets into a clear plan.

Calculate individual sports, build Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon strategies, measure key performance anchors, build an individualized hydration strategy, and organize your next race with Race Day Planner.

Run

Calculate pace, finish time, and distance. Build splits, review pace zones and race estimates, and save run plans.

Bike

Calculate speed, ride time, and distance. Build bike splits, compare ride estimates, and save bike plans.

Swim

Calculate swim pace, time, and distance in meters or yards. Build standard or custom splits and save swim plans.

Multisport

Plan Brick, Duathlon, and Triathlon events with independent discipline targets, transitions, race presets, custom distances, and complete target finish times.

Privacy First

EnduroPace does not require an account. Calculations happen locally on your device, and app settings, saved plans, Race Day Plans, Performance Lab measurements, Sweat Tests, and hydration preferences are designed to remain stored locally on your device.

EnduroPace is a planning and educational tool. Actual results may vary based on fitness, training, terrain, weather, elevation, equipment, fueling, hydration, fatigue, and race-day conditions.
