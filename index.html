Privacy

EnduroPace is designed around a local-first experience with optional anonymous usage analytics that are off by default.

Read Privacy Policy →

Private by design

EnduroPace does not require an account for the current app experience. App settings, saved calculator plans, Race Day Plans, and Performance Lab measurements are designed to use local persistence on your device.

Optional anonymous product-usage analytics can be enabled or disabled from Settings and are off by default.

About EnduroPace

EnduroPace is an endurance race-planning and performance app for running, cycling, swimming, triathlon, duathlon, and brick workouts. It combines pacing, Performance Lab measurements, race conditions, hydration, sodium, fueling, and race-day timeline planning.
